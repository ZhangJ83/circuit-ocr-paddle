1. SUMMARY OF THE PAPER:
The authors propose **CircuitOCR**, a visual language model (VLM) system for circuit schematic diagram understanding, built upon the PaddleOCR-VL-0.9B base architecture. The core methodology involves identifying and mitigating "Modality Collapse" caused by Projector LoRA fine-tuning through an LLM-only approach ($r=16$). A key contribution is the construction of a new benchmark dataset ("V5 Golden"), comprising 2,555 samples with deterministic ground truth (zero AI annotation) from KiCad and Masala-CHAI sources. The authors claim to achieve significant performance improvements over the base model on string-level metrics (NED), training entirely within consumer-grade hardware constraints (RTX 4060).

2. MAIN STRENGTHS:
*   **Novel Insight into VLM Degradation:** The identification of "Modality Collapse" as a root cause when fine-tuning the Projector layer is a significant technical contribution to the broader VLM community, offering a safety mechanism (freezing visual encoders) for downstream tasks.
*   **Dataset Quality & Construction:** The creation of the "V5 Golden" dataset with explicit rules for Ground Truth generation (SVG text extraction vs SPICE parsing) addresses common issues in existing circuit datasets regarding format mismatch and noise.
*   **Open-Source Ecosystem:** Full transparency is provided via GitHub, Hugging Face weights, and a live demo, which significantly aids reproducibility compared to typical black-box submissions.
*   **Low-VRAM Efficiency:** Demonstrating effective fine-tuning on an 8GB consumer GPU (RTX 4060) without Flash Attention suggests practical utility for resource-constrained environments in EDA workflows.

3. WEAKNESSES & CONCERNS:
*   **Critical Metric Ambiguity/Confusion:** The paper reports Base NED = 0.9372 vs Fine-tuned NED = 0.7760 with a claim of "error rate reduction." Standard Normalized Edit Distance (NED) typically implies Lower is Better (closer to 0). However, the phrasing suggests an improvement where performance increased while the metric value decreased? Or does it imply Accuracy where Higher is better but they inverted the numbers? The definition formula for NED must be explicitly stated in a table or appendix. Without this, "Error Rate Reduction" contradicts standard interpretation of high-value metrics (e.g., if 0.93 was Base and 0.77 is Fine-tuned on an Accuracy scale, performance *worsened*). This ambiguity undermines the validity of the claimed improvement.
*   **Missing Topological Evaluation:** The introduction explicitly states that circuit understanding involves "topology graph theory tracking." However, Table \ref{tab:evolution} and Section 3 only report string-level NED scores (easy50/100). There is no quantitative evaluation of topological correctness (e.g., Pin Accuracy or Netlist F1) for the V8-Fixed model. For an EDA conference, text extraction alone does not constitute "Schematic Understanding."
*   **Dataset Size & Bias:** 2,555 samples is relatively small for training a large Vision-Language Model compared to state-of-the-art benchmarks (e.g., LLaVA-13B). The dataset lacks diversity in complex PCB layouts or dense routing scenarios common in industrial reverse engineering. "Zero AI annotation" claims need scrutiny: how were KiCad SVG text extractions verified against human ground truth?
*   **Hardware & Scalability Constraints:** Training on a single RTX 4060 (8GB VRAM) limits the generalizability of findings to production-grade hardware where Flash Attention is standard. The limitation regarding "Flash Attention unavailable" should be framed as an experimental constraint rather than just a weakness, but it raises questions about efficiency compared to A100/H100 baselines often expected in top-tier papers.
*   **Manuscript Formatting & Placeholders:** There are suspicious placeholder entries (e.g., `hippo@cs.cranberry-lemon.edu`, ORCID 0000-0000) within the LaTeX code, suggesting incomplete preparation or template artifacts that need cleaning before submission to ensure professional rigor.

4. CLARIFYING QUESTIONS FOR AUTHORS:
1.  **Metric Definition:** Please explicitly define the NED formula used in Section \ref{tab:evolution}. Is it $\frac{\text{EditDistance}}{|GT|}$ or $1 - \text{F1}$? How does a value of 0.9372 (Base) compare to 0.7760 (Fine-tuned)? Does the latter represent an improvement in accuracy, and if so, why is it lower than the base score on what appears to be a distance-based metric?
2.  **Topological Fidelity:** The paper claims "Schematic Diagram Understanding" but lacks topological evaluation metrics (e.g., Pin-to-Pin connectivity F1). Can you provide an ablation study comparing V8-Fixed against Base specifically on netlist reconstruction accuracy, not just text string matching?
3.  **Dataset Verification Protocol:** How was the GT extraction from KiCad SVGs validated to ensure "Zero AI annotation"? Was there a human-in-the-loop verification step for complex symbols (e.g., integrated circuits vs discrete components) that might be mis-rendered by OCR tools during generation?
4.  **Reproducibility of Collapse:** The Modality Collapse phenomenon is attributed to Projector LoRA tuning ($r=8$). Can you provide the specific checkpoint logs or gradient statistics showing how the LLM attention weights degenerate when the Projector is perturbed vs frozen, beyond just output diversity metrics?

5. OVERALL RATING: **Weak Accept**
*   **Decision Explanation:** The paper presents a compelling and novel technical insight regarding VLM fine-tuning stability (Modality Collapse) which has broad implications for Vision-Language models in engineering domains. The open-source nature of the work is highly commendable and aligns with modern EDA research trends. However, the critical ambiguity surrounding the performance metrics undermines confidence in the quantitative claims without immediate clarification. Furthermore, missing topological evaluation limits its relevance to an *EDA* conference specifically (as opposed to a general VLM conf). I recommend acceptance **with Major Revisions** requiring:
    1.  Clarification of NED metric definition and directionality.
    2.  Addition or at least discussion on Topology F1 scores in the main results table.
    3.  Removal of LaTeX placeholder artifacts (emails/ORCIDs) to ensure professional submission standards.
